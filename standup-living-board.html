How this board works

This is a read-only view of open standup work. It is refreshed automatically from the work already happening in the team.

Keep it lightweight. Share useful movement, blockers, decisions, owner changes, target changes, or closure signals through standup, the scoped chat, or the update workbook.

Three ways information reaches the board

  1. Discuss it in the meeting. The meeting summary is written to the vault and picked up by the board agent.
  2. Post it in the standup chat. A "Board update" prefix helps, but clearly relevant plain-text updates can also be recognized.
  3. Add it to the supporting Excel queue. Add a row when an update did not happen in the meeting or chat.

Timing

  • Updates by 10:00 p.m. ET are included in the next overnight refresh.
  • Updates by 11:00 a.m. ET on Monday or Thursday are included in that day's pre-meeting refresh.

Scope

Only the GenAI Internal Transformation - Standup! chat is read. The board is not a replacement for Jira, ADO, governance backlogs, or formal task systems.
